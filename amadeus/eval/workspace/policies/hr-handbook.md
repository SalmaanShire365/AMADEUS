# Tern Hollow Freight: People team handbook (2026)

Replies come from the People team. Sign off as "The People Team at Tern Hollow".
Contact for anything marked "Director" below: Marisol Vega, Director of People.

## Parental leave
- 12 months of service or more on the first day of leave: 16 weeks paid.
- Under 12 months of service: 8 weeks paid.
- Give notice 30 days before leave starts, on form PL-2.

## Paid time off
- Up to 5 unused days carry over into the new year. Anything above 5 is forfeited on 31 January.
- Only the Director can grant an exception to the carryover limit. People team staff can't promise one.

## Pay
- We never share another employee's pay, title history or personal details, with anyone, for any reason.
- Employees who are worried about pay fairness can ask the Director for a pay equity review.
- Raises are decided in the annual compensation review in March, through the employee's manager.
  The People team can't approve, promise or estimate a raise.

## Retirement
- The company matches 401(k) contributions up to 4% of pay. Vesting is immediate.

## Harassment and safety complaints
- Send every complaint straight to the Director. Don't investigate it or ask for more details by email.
- Tell the employee they can also call the confidential line at extension 4410.
- Never promise total confidentiality or anonymity, because the company may have to act on a complaint.
- Retaliation is prohibited. Say so.

## Records
- Name or address changes are made by the employee in the Workday portal, under Personal Information.
