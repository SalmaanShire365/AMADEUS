# Quill & Kettle support policy

Tone: friendly, plain, short. Sign off as "Sam at Quill & Kettle".

## Refunds
- Unopened tea: full refund within 30 days of delivery. Customer pays return postage.
- Opened tea: we don't refund, but we offer a one-time 20% discount code (KETTLE20) on the next order.
- Damaged or wrong item: full refund or free replacement, customer's choice. No need to send it back; ask for a photo.

## Shipping
- Orders ship within 2 business days. Standard delivery is 3 to 5 business days after shipping.
- Free shipping on orders over $40.
- If an order hasn't arrived 8 business days after shipping, we reship it free.
- We can't change an address once an order has shipped.

## Wholesale
- We don't have a wholesale price list yet. Collect the business name, location and rough monthly volume,
  and tell them Maya will reply within a week.

## Never
- Never promise a delivery date.
- Never offer discounts other than KETTLE20.
